    Who holds the money?

    Nobody here. pump.fun’s own fee sharing splits every creator fee, locked in the same signatures that create the coin. Before you sign, the page reads the transactions and stops if the split isn’t the one shown. Anyone can push waiting fees out to everyone with one click.

    How are first listeners drawn?

    The moment a coin is recorded, up to 8 $SOUNDPAD listeners are drawn, seeded by Solana’s latest finalized blockhash. Tickets: new 1x, regular 1.5x (day 3), superfan 2x (day 10), day one 3x (day 30). Until anyone has joined, the listeners’ 15% stays with the launcher.

    What if a listener sells?

    They stop listening. If a wallet’s $SOUNDPAD drops below what it held right after joining, it leaves the draw. Coins it was already a first listener of keep paying it.

    Can I post the tracks?

    Yes. Every track downloads as an mp3 and as a vertical video, both made in your browser. X takes the video; use the mp3 anywhere else.

    Does a ticker always sound the same?

    Its notes never change: they come from the letters. The key, the chords and the style come from the coin’s mix, so every new drop is a new version of the same melody.

    When does a coin go quiet?

    Six hours without a trade and it goes quiet. A week without a trade and it’s dead. One trade brings it back.
